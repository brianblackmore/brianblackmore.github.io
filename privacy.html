Theatre Calendar

Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Theatre Calendar, maintained by Brian Blackmore, displays calendar information about theatre trips. This policy explains the purpose of that data use and what happens when you visit this website.

Calendar information used

The app uses theatre-trip calendar data: event titles and details, dates and times, and theatre or venue locations. Event details and locations may contain personal information if it has been included in the source calendar.

How calendar information is used

Calendar information is used to display theatre events and their locations. It is not sold or used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Displaying calendar information exposes it to the people who can access that display. Do not include private addresses, booking references, or other sensitive information in calendar entries intended to be shared publicly.

Google Calendar access

If you authorize the app to access Google Calendar, that access is used for the theatre-calendar display described above. You can review or revoke access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not remove information that someone has already viewed or copied.

This website and hosting

This homepage and privacy policy are static pages. They do not ask you to sign in, submit calendar information, or provide payment details, and they contain no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Managing your information

You can edit or remove theatre-trip information in the source calendar. If information has been shared publicly, removing the source entry cannot recall copies made by others. For questions about the app’s handling of your calendar information or requests to remove a displayed entry, contact the developer through Brian Blackmore’s GitHub profile. Do not post private calendar information in public messages.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app’s data use changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
