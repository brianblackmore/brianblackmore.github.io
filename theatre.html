Theatre Notes

Theatre app and calendar features

Theatre Calendar works with a personal theatre-booking workbook to help prepare, check, and share upcoming plans.

Keep companion calendars up to date.

Theatre Calendar syncs each configured companion's upcoming theatre bookings to a dedicated Google Calendar, updating changes and removing bookings that are no longer planned.

Preparing calendar events

Upcoming workbook bookings are selected for each configured companion. The event title and attending people are included. When a matching event with a time is found in a source calendar, its start and end times are used. Otherwise the booking is an all-day event. Its location comes from a matching event when available, or from the venue and city recorded in the workbook.

Cancelled bookings and reservation placeholders are left out. Tentative bookings are marked as tentative. Person-specific iCalendar (.ics) exports are also available for import.

Checking plans

Theatre bookings are also compared with personal and shared source calendars. The checks can flag bookings missing from a calendar, unmatched calendar events, duplicate entries, possible overlaps, and certain timing or travel issues. Edinburgh Fringe bookings are handled separately.

Google Calendar sync

  • Maintain a separate app-owned Google Calendar for each configured companion.
  • Use the narrow calendar.app.created permission, which applies to calendars created by this app rather than pre-existing Google calendars.
  • Add and update upcoming bookings, remove managed bookings that are no longer planned, and retain past events.
  • The calendar owner shares a calendar manually with read-only event access; calendars are not made public.
